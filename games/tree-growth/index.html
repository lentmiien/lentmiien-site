in your own time.

Meet the giant sequoia, an evergreen native to California’s Sierra Nevada. Its fibrous red bark, enormous trunk, and high crown make it an extraordinary record of time.

Make yourself at home

  • Grow or pause: use the play button, or press Space while exploring the scene.
  • Change time: move the speed slider at any time. Scrub the lifetime or tap a year to visit it; seeking pauses growth.
  • Explore: drag to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom. Shift-drag, right-drag, or two-finger drag to move up and down. The height slider and arrow buttons do the same.
  • Keyboard: focus the scene, then use arrow keys to orbit, W / S to move vertically, + / − to zoom, and F to fit the tree.

Watch the world get smaller

The ground markings, stones, and 1.8-metre figure stay the same size as your tree grows. Branches unfold at new heights while older wood thickens in place. The camera holds still between occasional pullbacks. Use the padlock to hold its framing, even as the tree outgrows the view; Fit releases it.

A portrait, not a prediction

This is an idealized 3,000-year lifetime in favorable conditions, paced to keep height and branch growth visible throughout. Height, base diameter, canopy, and carbon follow a simplified model. Carbon estimates only the dry trunk’s stored carbon. Real trees respond to weather, competition, fire, and much more.
