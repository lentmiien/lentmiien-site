# Lumenwild: The Folded Garden

You are Pip, the last lantern keeper. A sleeping garden has folded into two overlapping worlds. Recover three heartseeds in each region and return them to its waygate. Restore the Verdant Hollows, the Moonwell Archive, and the Glassbloom Conservatory to wake the world-tree.

## Play loop

Explore a generated labyrinth → find treasure, memories, and heartseeds → shift the maze between Bloom and Echo → defeat or evade telegraphed enemies → solve optional rune sequences → select an expedition blessing → restore the next region → bring starlight home to improve the camp.

The world reacts only when you take an action. Animation, lighting, and sound remain alive while you think. Bumping a wall, checking the map, or opening the journal costs no turn. No timer or endlessly spawning enemies. Lantern attacks are unlimited; damage briefly stuns foes. Dash, phase shift, bombs, medicine, and a route-finding thread offer multiple solutions.

## Variety and progression

- Three distinct regions with different art, enemy mixes, journal discoveries, and a finite ending.
- Connected procedural mazes, hand-shaped clearings, optional phase passages, treasure caches, and optional sequence shrines.
- Choose one of three blessings at each restored waygate; combinations change combat, exploration, and healing.
- An optional daily expedition uses a UTC date seed and a standard loadout. Regular expeditions benefit from persistent camp upgrades. A gentle mode lowers damage and increases supplies.
- Persistent discovered memories, wins, and camp starlight. An expedition autosaves; continue after closing the page. No accounts or network calls.

## Controls

WASD/arrows move; Space lantern sweep; Q shift world; Shift dash in facing direction; E interact; 1 medicine; 2 seedbomb; 3 guiding thread; M map; J journal; Escape pause. On-screen directional and ability controls support touch. Holding movement repeats at a deliberately readable rate.

## Visual direction

An original hand-painted storybook world: amber fox, teal traveling cloak, warm lantern, ancient botanical mechanisms, luminous mushrooms, and mossy stone. Generated art includes the title illustration, eight fox poses, twelve creature frames, sixteen props, and sixteen terrain tiles. UI uses the shared Graphite/Ember/Golden Amber tokens. Rendering uses Canvas 2D with fog, soft lighting, footsteps, particles, world-transition effects, and camera smoothing; no external engine or runtime asset service.
