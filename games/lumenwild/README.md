# Lumenwild · The Returning Light

A standalone maze adventure about a lantern fox restoring a forgotten botanical world. **Play at `/lumenwild/`**, or find Lumenwild in `/games`.

No app-route changes, database migration, environment variables, dependency installation, or build step are required. The existing game directory mount serves this folder; all resources are local.

## The adventure

Eight chapters open twenty-three regions. Recover three heartseeds in each region and return to its waygate. Guardian regions also require an encounter at their arena. Every expedition saves between sessions, including between strikes in a boss fight.

| Journey | Regions | Distinct exploration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The First Light | Verdant Hollows, Moonwell Archive, Glassbloom Conservatory | Winding hedges, library aisles, and courtyards; root lanterns, memory lenses, and glass blossoms |
| The Amber Frontier | Tideweave Marsh, Saffron Orchard, Clockwork Cloister | Ferry landings, branching orchard groves, and connected clockwork chambers |
| The Far Horizon | Frostglass Grotto, Cinderroot Caldera, Astral Canopy | Crystal caves, caldera rings, and island bridges; prisms, cooling hearts, and ordered star anchors |
| The Rainbound Wilds | Sunken Belfry, Mycelium Warrens, Stormglass Causeway | Concentric flooded ruins, connected mushroom chambers, and narrow causeways; the Bellkeeper and Tempest Roc |
| Where Dawn Begins | Sundial Desert, Velvet Labyrinth, Aurora Crown | Dune rings, woven hedge lanes, and crystal terraces; sun dials, silk lanterns, and the Dawn Hart |
| **The Hidden Seasons** | **Honeyglass Apiary, Paperwind Reach, Singing Quarry** | **Wax chambers, paper islands, and fault galleries; nectar fountains, crane travel, ordered chimes, the Honey Regent and Resonant Warden** |
| **The Unwritten Sky** | **Lanternwake Bazaar, Mirrorsea Observatory, Lastlight Orchard** | **Covered market lanes, circular orbits, and branching root roads; market lanterns, moon dials, evening saplings and the Night Gardener** |
| **The Returning Light** | **Dawnroot Approach, Heart of the Garden** | **Braided root bridges, petal chambers, homeward beacons, ordered heart blossoms, and the First Keeper** |
| The Long Way Home | The original nine regions | A saved pilgrimage with eight blessing choices |
| All the Little Lights | The first fifteen regions | A saved homecoming with fourteen distinct blessings and the original three guardians |
| **The Long Tomorrow** | **The six Unwritten Seasons regions in an alternating route** | **A saved expedition with five blessings and all three new guardians** |

The First Light opens The Amber Frontier. Finishing the Frontier opens both the Far Horizon and the Rainbound Wilds. Rainbound opens Where Dawn Begins, which opens both All the Little Lights and **The Hidden Seasons**. The Hidden Seasons opens The Unwritten Sky; completing that chapter opens **The Returning Light** and the optional Long Tomorrow. The Returning Light is the two-region final chapter, with an illustrated ending that can be replayed at camp. The original nine-region pilgrimage still follows the Far Horizon. Any unlocked region can be visited individually; completed regions offer optional tier II and III surveys.

## The waking guardians

Collect a region’s three heartseeds, then approach its arena and press **E**. The introduction explains the guardian before the fight begins; hearts and focus refill. Restore the region’s three landmarks first for longer attack warnings.

| Guardian | Patterns | Gifts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Bellkeeper | Alternating bell lanes, rising tide rings, realm-marked echoes | Bellwater pulse art; Tidekeeper’s vow heals after a successful guard |
| The Tempest Roc | A broad flight lane, targeted feathers, crosswinds; lands near you after a dive | Storm feather art; Windkeeper’s ribbon speeds dash recovery |
| The Dawn Hart | Branching roots, a crown of light, realm-marked sky attacks | Daybreak art; The dawn seed adds maximum hearts and starting seedbombs |
| **The Honey Regent** | **Staggered honeycomb warnings, two crossing flight lanes, targeted nectar followed by a surrounding ring** | **Summer shelter art; Nectar locket adds focus and a shield when drinking a tonic** |
| **The Resonant Warden** | **An inner shockwave followed by an outer aftershock, crossing fault lines, alternating-realm echoes** | **Mountain song art; Listening stone shortens guard recovery** |
| **The Night Gardener** | **Crossing shadows, a safe island that changes sides, opposite-realm attacks with safe lanes** | **The evening light art; Light for later shortens fold recovery and extends its shield** |
| **The First Keeper** | **Three-strike root lanes, a lantern procession, and alternating realms before a closing ring** | **First light deals six damage and resets dash/fold; The home lantern extends sweep reach during recovery** |

All seven story guardians have three phases. A committed attack continues when you stand still, fold, attack, or cast an art. Read the marked ground and its countdown, then dodge or guard. A Bloom/Echo symbol means the attack only hurts in that realm, and every pattern also leaves safe ground. **Open-heart recovery windows take double damage.** Phase boundaries prevent a supply burst from skipping the rest of the encounter.

The Honey Regent, Resonant Warden and Night Gardener attack **twice before resting**. The First Keeper attacks **three times**; the dotted preview always shows the next strike’s number. Solid warnings and a countdown show the current strike; dotted lavender tiles marked **2**, then **3** for the First Keeper, preview the follow-up. The banner names each strike's realm separately. Each follow-up gets its own countdown; recovery begins after the final strike. Gentle mode and restored regional landmarks lengthen each warning.

A failed attempt returns you to the entrance with full hearts and the exact supplies you entered with. The maze, findings, and expedition progress remain. The pause menu also lets you leave voluntarily. Victory restores hearts and focus, grants a tonic and starlight, and unlocks the guardian’s art and charm.

**Guardian trials** in the world atlas take you straight to a previously awakened guardian. Three tiers offer more resolve and shorter warnings, with no repeated heartseed hunt. Each first-earned seal awards 40 starlight. Trials do not advance regional survey progress.

## The last hidden path

Collect **all 138 memories, 69 regional relics, 13 lantern arts and eight collectible keepsakes** to reveal **The Unlit Between** at camp. The journal and world atlas show progress for each group and how to earn missing equipment. The journal's missing-only filter and regional visit buttons help finish the collection. Consumables, currency, camp upgrade levels, medals and vaults are not part of the gate; no hidden-boss reward is required to unlock itself.

The **Hollow Star** is the final mastery encounter: **152 resolve, four phases, four committed strikes per sequence, and an armored heart that only takes damage during recovery**. Moving safe lanes, rotating islands and realm changes build on earlier fights. Early warnings are 1.65 seconds, follow-ups 1.15 seconds and openings 2.1 seconds; by the last phase these shrink to 1.41, 0.97 and 1.8 seconds. Hits deal three hearts, rising to four in the final phase. This is deliberately harder than a radiant story-guardian trial.

Your camp upgrades, chosen art and charm remain equipped. The stage supplies **two tonics and two seedbombs**, uses fixed mastery difficulty, and explicitly disables Gentle mode. Failed attempts restore the entry kit; pausing, saving mid-sequence and leaving the arena remain available. Victory earns the permanent **Unfading Keeper** title, 120 starlight, and local records for clears, fastest time and fewest hits. Replay the stage to improve them. It has no survey medals or hidden collection items.

## More reasons to wander

- **Realm-key vaults:** in each of the eight regions from Honeyglass Apiary onward, collect a sun key in Bloom and a moon key in Echo. Either key marks the vault on the atlas. Bring both to the vault and press E for starlight, seedbombs, healing, focus, and nearby shortcuts. The world atlas records which regions' vaults you have found.
- **Wishbeds:** plant near camp for four expedition starlight, then walk 24 tiles while exploring. Return for a tonic, three hearts, full focus, and five starlight. Walking and dashing grow the plant; waiting, attacking and arena movement do not. Growth is saved, and each bed can be harvested once per visit.
- **Wandering wayfarer:** trade expedition starlight for a tonic bundle, bombs and thread, or a discovery chart that reveals objectives and refills focus. One purchase per visit; camp savings are not spent.
- **Lost fireflies:** carry a firefly to the waygate for 12 starlight, a tonic, and two hearts. Three rescues unlock a charm with extra focus capacity and starting threads. A carried firefly gets priority in the guiding thread.
- **Fold looms:** interact to swap nearby Bloom and Echo shortcuts. Affected tiles appear on the atlas. Ordinary routes remain connected, and a loom cannot close a path beneath you.
- **Dreamcap and paper-crane travel:** restored mushrooms transport you through the warrens’ roots; restored cranes unfold routes between Paperwind's islands. Landmark secrets open shortcuts and help with guardian preparations.
- **138 memories and 69 relics:** each region has six pages and three relics. New visits prefer undiscovered finds; full exploration across three visits can complete each region’s collection.
- **69 survey medals:** bronze, silver, and gold combine completed visits, exploration, collections, and landmark secrets. Each medal awards 25 starlight.
- **27 permanent upgrade levels, thirteen arts, eight collectible charms, and 16 expedition blessings.** Equip one art and one charm at camp for the next expedition. A continuing expedition keeps its original equipment.
- **Daily journeys:** a UTC-date seed selects three regions from across the twenty-three-region world. Loadouts and discoveries are independent of camp upgrades, charms, collections, arts, and gentle settings. Scores stay local.

## Combat and controls

Twenty-three ordinary enemy types use bites, targeted shots, slams, lanes, crosses, fans, and bursts. The new regions add honeyglass drones, inkwing cranes, copper chimebacks, lantern tanuki, mirrorfin rays, and twilight hares. The final gardens add root pangolins and heartblossom cubs. Their warning rings count down in real time. Ordinary sweeps do not interrupt them; folding, bombs, and arts can. Guardians resist these interruptions. Guard reflects either kind of attack. A short damage grace period prevents simultaneous hits.

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Move; hold to continue walking |
| Space | Lantern sweep |
| G / R | Timed mirror guard / equipped art |
| Q / Shift | Fold between Bloom and Echo / dash in your facing direction |
| E | Interact with discoveries, traders, looms, arenas, and waygates |
| 1 / 2 / 3 | Tonic / seedbomb / guiding thread |
| M / J / Escape | Atlas / journal / pause |
| Touch | Direction pad, swipes, and ten action buttons |

Every menu, reading dialog, hidden tab, and focus loss pauses combat. No offline time is replayed. Gentle journeys add hearts and tonics, lengthen warnings, and limit incoming damage to one heart; the optional hidden mastery encounter has its own fixed rules. Reduced-motion preferences disable decorative movement. Optional sound uses Web Audio.

## Saves and compatibility

The existing localStorage key, `lumenwild.garden.v1`, now stores **version 5** data. Camp upgrades, starlight, collection identifiers, and previous chapter records retain their meanings. Unfinished version-one and version-two expeditions use frozen generators. Version-three and version-four expeditions retain their original maps, encounters and daily routes, including active multi-strike guardian fights. The final regions become available on new expeditions after The Unwritten Sky. The collection gate is checked on both hidden-stage launch and resume.

Actions autosave after a short debounce; active combat checkpoints every two seconds. Page exit and returning to camp flush immediately. Guardian phase, current and previewed warning footprints, the bounded remaining-strike queue, current strike, landing targets, retry supplies, loom orientation, firefly state, keys, opened vaults and wishbed growth persist. Discoveries, guardian ranks, vault discoveries and delivered fireflies sync to camp; starlight and completed surveys are banked on victory, defeat, or early return. Rewards cannot be awarded twice by reloading.

Progress stays in this browser. Storage failures show a notice without preventing play. Reset requires confirmation and affects only Lumenwild. There are no runtime external services, tracking, uploads, HTTP mutations, account integrations, or database operations. See [the security contract](docs/SECURITY.md).

## Art and implementation

The finale adds **45 generated WebP assets**: 16 terrain tiles, eight creature poses, 12 props, eight boss poses and an illustrated ending. They total 1,544,830 bytes. The complete game has **389 WebP assets, approximately 9.81 MiB**. Artwork used the imagegen skill's built-in generation/editing tool; grid extraction, resizing and WebP packaging used Sharp. See [finale prompts and asset record](assets/FINALE-ASSETS.md), [seasons artwork](assets/SEASONS-ASSETS.md), [guardian artwork](assets/GUARDIAN-ASSETS.md), [previous expansion art](assets/EXPANSION-ASSETS.md), and [original art](assets/GENERATED-ASSETS.md).

- `js/content.js`: all region, story, creature, chapter, guardian, art, and charm definitions.
- `js/core.js`: shared geometry/random helpers, frozen version-one generator, and entry point.
- `js/world-v2.js`: frozen version-two map generator.
- `js/world.js`: region layouts, encounters, and isolated trial arenas.
- `js/guardians.js`: guardian patterns, phases, recovery, retry, and save validation.
- `js/engine.js`: progression, combat, pathfinding, encounters, and versioned saves.
- `js/render.js`: Canvas artwork, fog, effects, warning shapes, and regional atlas.
- `js/game.js`: input, dialogs, camp, world atlas, workshop, journal, saving, and sound.

Use repository-pinned Node **26.13.0**:

```sh
npm test -- tests/unit/lumenwildGame.test.js tests/unit/lumenwildExpansion.test.js tests/unit/lumenwildGuardians.test.js tests/unit/lumenwildSeasons.test.js tests/unit/lumenwildFinale.test.js tests/unit/lumenwildUi.test.js --coverage=false
```

[Validation notes](docs/VALIDATION.md) distinguish simulation and DOM checks from browser checks. The optional Playwright smoke test uses a localhost-only static fixture, never app.js or prestart:

```sh
PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE=/path/to/playwright CHROMIUM_PATH=/path/to/chrome node tests/browser/lumenwild.smoke.cjs
PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE=/path/to/playwright CHROMIUM_PATH=/path/to/chrome node tests/browser/lumenwild.finale.cjs
```

It writes screenshots and results to `/tmp/lumenwild-review` (`LUMENWILD_REVIEW_OUTPUT` overrides this). The finale review writes to `/tmp/lumenwild-finale-review`; it covers both final gardens, the ending, hidden collection gates, a hidden mid-sequence reload, mastery victory, and phone layouts. For a manual preview from the repository root:

```sh
node -e "const express = require('express'); const app = express(); app.use('/css', express.static('public/css')); app.use('/lumenwild', express.static('games/lumenwild')); app.listen(8098, '127.0.0.1');"
```

Open `http://127.0.0.1:8098/lumenwild/`; stop with Ctrl+C.
