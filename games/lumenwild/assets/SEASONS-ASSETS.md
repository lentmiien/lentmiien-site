# The Unwritten Seasons: artwork record

Created with the **imagegen skill in built-in tool mode**. Ten generated sheets/illustrations were inspected, then mechanically cropped and resized with Sharp. Generated alpha is preserved; no existing art is replaced.

## Shipped files

- `tile-80..95.webp`: apiary, paper islands, quarry, and supporting arena floors.
- `tile-96..111.webp`: bazaar, observatory, orchard, and supporting floors.
- `enemy-60..83.webp`: six creatures with idle, moving, warning, and freed poses.
- `prop-80..97.webp`: eighteen new regional relics.
- `prop-98..103.webp`: six regional landmarks.
- `prop-104..107.webp`: Bloom key, Echo key, realm vault, and wishbed.
- `prop-108..111.webp`: three guardian gifts and the travelling lantern.
- `boss-honeyregent-0..3.webp`, `boss-resonantwarden-0..3.webp`, `boss-nightgardener-0..3.webp`: idle, warning, recovery, and awakened guardian poses.
- `seasons-atlas.webp`: six-region illustrated atlas.

There are **101 new WebP files**, totalling **2,605,662 bytes**. Terrain and small sprites are 192 × 192; guardian poses are 384 × 384; the atlas is 1536 × 1024. A dark-background contact sheet was used to inspect alpha edges and the final small silhouettes.
