# Generated Kardashev artwork

These nine project assets were created with the built-in image-generation tool for this experience. The generated 1672×941 PNG originals remain in the Codex generated-image store; application deliverables were resampled with Lanczos filtering to local 1600×900 WebP files at quality 86. All scenes were requested without captions, labels, signatures, logos, or watermarks.

Every scene is composed around a center-safe subject. The application uses `50% 50%` focal positioning for each image; no scene-specific override is required for the tested desktop and mobile crops.

## `first-signal.webp`

- **Purpose:** Opening and 1964 origin scene.
- **Prompt:** Historical-scene; a photorealistic cinematic interpretation of a young 1964 radio astronomer at an analog console, viewed from behind through observatory glass toward a large parabolic dish at blue dusk. Period bakelite controls, paper charts without readable writing, worn concrete, graphite-blue night, and amber instrument lamps. Widescreen, center-safe, with dark edge space. No digital screens, flags, text, labels, retrofuturism, logos, or watermark.
- **Dimensions / focal:** 1600×900 WebP; center 50% 50%.
- **Caveat:** Historically informed artistic reconstruction. It is not a portrait of Nikolai Kardashev, a documented room, or a photograph of the paper’s creation.

## `humanity-night.webp`

- **Purpose:** Humanity’s K ≈ 0.73 baseline and first discovery callback.
- **Prompt:** Scientific-educational; a physically realistic Earth at night from low orbit, with atmosphere, weather, plausible continent-scale city light, scattered small satellites, dark ocean, and an ember sunrise. NASA-documentary-inspired photorealism, center-safe composition, quiet edge space. No labels, neon circuit planet, impossible satellite density, logos, or watermark.
- **Dimensions / focal:** 1600×900 WebP; center 50% 50%.
- **Caveat:** Artist impression, not satellite imagery. Light intensity is compositionally heightened to make present energy infrastructure legible.
