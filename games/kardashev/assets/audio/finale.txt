Pull the camera home. Planet. Star. Galaxy. Each word expanded until human history looked like a spark, but the spark is where every future begins. Type I asks whether power can preserve the world that produces it. Type II asks whether industry can grow into space without turning complexity into catastrophe. Type III asks whether purpose can survive distance when there is no single now. None of those futures is guaranteed, and bigger is not automatically better. Yet we are not waiting at the bottom of an imaginary ladder. We are already holding its first rung. Every safer grid, longer-lived battery, restored wetland, asteroid survey, autonomous repair system, and patient telescope is part of the answer. The first interstellar civilization, if one ever exists, begins long before its first starship. It begins when a species learns to care across generations. Take one last look at the horizon. The next great technosignature the universe notices could be ours—and the story of what it means is still completely unwritten.
