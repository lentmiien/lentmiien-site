      FIELD GUIDE

      How to play

      01

      Explore

      Move with WASD or the arrow keys. On touch devices, use the directional pad. Amber markers identify people, objects, and routes you can inspect.

      02

      Interact

      Press E, Enter, or Space near a marker. The same keys advance dialogue. Esc opens Pause or closes the top panel.

      03

      Choose

      Use arrow keys and Enter, or number keys 1–4. Dangerous options are foreshadowed and explicitly marked. There are no hidden combat statistics.

      04

      Orient

      Open Map in the exploration header or press M. Travel interludes mark where Aren leaves, where he arrives, and the route taken across the Hand.

      05

      Recover

      The game saves after scenes and map transitions. Checkpoints appear before major risks. A game over always offers a checkpoint retry and explains the warning that was ignored.

      Story encounters, not combat

      Investigate, protect, hide, retreat, or use a clue. The point is to choose a consequence, not guess an invisible damage number.

      ABOUT THE ADVENTURE

      Credits & disclosures

      Source and adaptation

      Adapted from the Swedish concept outline in games/lentmanc_game/idea.txt. The seven crystals, the Hand, the two dimensions, the king’s motive, the wounded knight, the airship, and the three outcome structures come from that source.

      Asterra, Veyra, named characters, detailed locations, civilian stories, connective scenes, dialogue, and encounter mechanics were created for this playable English adaptation.

      Generated media

      Character references, portraits, title art, the painted world chart, cut-scene illustrations, and ending art were generated specifically for this game and reviewed against written continuity rules. They are artist’s interpretations of a fictional world.

      Place labels, travel routes, current-location markers, exploration maps, collision, tiles, props, and sprites are deterministic local HTML, SVG, and canvas drawings. No third-party media URL is used at runtime.

      Voice

      Aren uses the local Piper Lessac English voice. The narrator uses the distinct local Piper Amy English voice. Every other character is intentionally text-only.

      WAV masters, browser-ready MP3s, and exact English transcripts ship locally. Voice playback can be muted or prevented from starting automatically.

      Design and engineering

      Story design, English adaptation, UI, accessibility, maps, save system, and implementation were produced for this repository. The interface follows the site’s Graphite, Ember, and Golden Amber color system.

      Detailed creative additions and continuity rules are documented in docs/GAME-DESIGN.md and docs/CHARACTER-BIBLE.md.
