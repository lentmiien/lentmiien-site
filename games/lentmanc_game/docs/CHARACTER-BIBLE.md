# The great adventure — Character Bible

## Shared art rules

All recurring characters belong to one anime-inspired painted-2D production style: confident clean linework, soft cel-shaded faces, lightly textured cloth and leather, realistic material highlights, readable silhouettes, and cinematic but restrained lighting. Proportions are grounded rather than super-deformed in portraits and illustrations; exploration sprites simplify each silhouette to roughly two heads tall.

No generated image may contain captions, labels, signatures, watermarks, logos, pseudo-writing, photoreal faces, modern objects, guns, excessive armor ornament, or costume redesigns. Graphite shadows, Ember practical light, Golden Amber crystal light, forest green, and Veyran cyan form the shared palette.

## Aren Vale

- **Identity:** he/him; playable protagonist; 19; an ordinary Willowmere villager forced into a world-level decision.
- **Personality and motivation:** observant, earnest, dryly funny when calm, and impulsive when grief is touched. He begins wanting revenge for his parents and gradually learns to distinguish justice from punishment. His central relationship is with Bram, who becomes a surrogate uncle; he distrusts Cael’s omissions, respects Mira’s practical courage, and is initially intimidated by Mara.
- **Speaking style:** short concrete sentences, occasional understated joke, questions before speeches. Anger makes him speak too quickly. Determination makes him quieter, not louder.
- **Build:** 174 cm; lean, wiry, long-legged, narrow shoulders that square over the story. Hands show small scratches from farm and forest work.
- **Face:** soft angular oval; warm light-brown skin; wide amber-brown eyes; straight dark eyebrows with a small notch in the right brow; compact straight nose; faint freckles across the nose; no facial hair. Distinguishing feature: a tiny crescent scar under the left jaw.
- **Hair:** thick, slightly wavy chestnut-brown hair; uneven ear-length layers; three fixed pointed locks over the forehead; longer nape; never tied and never black, blond, or bright red.
- **Clothing:** charcoal-blue collarless linen shirt; short moss-green hooded travel jacket with rolled sleeves; rust-orange woven scarf; dark brown fitted trousers; weathered tan belt and cross-body mushroom satchel; calf-high brown leather boots. After Willowmere, a narrow pale-gray memorial ribbon is permanently tied around the left wrist. No armor.
- **Props:** woven mushroom satchel; small brass tinder case; later carries one dull crystal shard in a padded cloth pouch as evidence, never as a weapon.
- **Posture and expression:** curious forward lean; rubs the memorial ribbon when worried; shoulders tense and fists close when angry; determined expression is level-eyed with relaxed mouth rather than a heroic shout.
- **Portrait framing:** chest-up, three-quarter turn toward screen center, scarf and left-wrist ribbon visible when possible. Golden-amber rim light may appear around crystal scenes.
- **Sprite silhouette:** chestnut hair triangle, orange scarf pixel/block, moss-green torso, tan satchel diagonal, brown boots.
- **Invariants:** amber-brown eyes; warm light-brown skin and freckles; three forehead locks; moss jacket; rust scarf; mushroom satchel; right-brow notch; left-jaw crescent scar; memorial ribbon after the prologue.
- **Allowed changes:** soot and torn sleeve after Willowmere; snow mantle at Frostcrown; expressions; damp hair in rain; minor bruising at the climax.
- **Expression set:** neutral/curious, small relieved smile, worried, determined, surprised, hurt, controlled anger.
- **Reusable generation description:** “Aren Vale, a lean 19-year-old fantasy villager with warm light-brown freckled skin, amber-brown eyes, a notched right eyebrow, tiny crescent scar under the left jaw, and thick slightly wavy chestnut ear-length hair with exactly three pointed forehead locks. He wears a charcoal-blue collarless shirt, short moss-green hooded jacket with rolled sleeves, rust-orange woven scarf, dark brown trousers, tan cross-body mushroom satchel, brown boots, and a pale-gray memorial ribbon on his left wrist. Grounded anime-inspired painted 2D character design, clean expressive linework, restrained cel shading.”
- **Negative constraints:** no sword, armor, cape, modern clothes, blond or black hair, blue eyes, pale skin, facial hair, muscular bodybuilder build, scarf color change, missing satchel, missing brow notch, childlike proportions, text, watermark.

## Bram Alder

- **Identity:** he/him; woodcutter and former border militia fighter; 45.
- **Personality and motivation:** patient, physically capable, grief-worn, and protective. His wife Sella and daughter Piri died when Mossreach was destroyed during the first crystal restoration. He wants the king stopped but recognizes his own revenge in Aren and refuses to let the boy inherit it.
- **Relationships:** surrogate uncle to Aren; initially hostile to Cael; gentle with Mira; trades engineering jokes with Mara; once trained with palace guards and knows their discipline.
- **Speaking style:** spare rural metaphors, low-key humor, never ornate. Pauses before saying the names of his family.
- **Build:** 188 cm; broad and heavy through shoulders and forearms; sturdy rather than sculpted; slightly stooped from years of chopping.
- **Face:** square weathered face; medium-brown skin; deep-set gray-green eyes; heavy straight brows; broad broken nose; two-day dark beard with gray at the chin; old pale cut across the bridge of the nose.
- **Hair:** dark umber, cropped close at sides, wavy on top, gray at temples. Never long or fully shaved.
- **Clothing:** faded brick-red wool overshirt, charcoal undershirt, dark forest-green work vest, broad leather belt, brown work trousers, iron-toed boots, one fingerless glove on the right hand. A small carved wooden bird from Piri hangs at his neck.
- **Props:** short-handled woodsman’s axe kept bound during travel and used as a tool or defensive prop, not a combat system; coil of rope.
- **Posture and expression:** plants himself between danger and others; folds arms when skeptical; smile is rare and lopsided.
- **Portrait framing:** chest-up, three-quarter toward center, wooden bird visible.
- **Sprite silhouette:** tall broad body, dark flat-topped hair, brick torso, green vest, axe/rope block at back.
- **Invariants:** broad build; broken nose and nose-bridge scar; gray-green eyes; brick shirt; green vest; wooden bird necklace; right glove; bound axe.
- **Allowed changes:** ash, bandaged shoulder after Cinder Thumb, snow cloak, expression.
- **Expression set:** guarded neutral, rueful smile, worried, protective determination, grief, anger.
- **Reusable generation description:** “Bram Alder, a broad sturdy 45-year-old woodcutter and former militia fighter with medium-brown skin, square weathered face, deep gray-green eyes, heavy brows, broad broken nose with a pale bridge scar, short dark-umber wavy hair graying at the temples, and a short beard gray at the chin. Faded brick-red wool overshirt, charcoal undershirt, dark forest-green work vest, work trousers, iron-toed boots, one right fingerless glove, small carved wooden bird necklace, bound short-handled axe and rope. Grounded anime-inspired painted 2D.”
- **Negative constraints:** no giant battle axe, shining plate armor, helmet, blond hair, clean-shaven face, slim build, eyepatch, modern lumberjack plaid, missing necklace, text, watermark.

## Sir Cael Varin

- **Identity:** he/him; Veyran royal knight and keeper of the removed crystals; 37.
- **Personality and motivation:** disciplined, intelligent, stoic, and capable of manipulation when he believes the stakes justify it. He accepted a one-way mission to seal the gate after Veyra’s seer predicted catastrophe. Wounded and trapped, he uses Aren before admitting his origin.
- **Relationships:** respects Bram’s judgment but clashes with him over means; sees Aren first as a necessary agent and later as a moral equal; knows King Edric only through pursuit; carries a sealed oath from Veyra’s king.
- **Speaking style:** exact, formal, never chatty. Avoids direct lies by narrowing answers. After his confession his sentences become simpler and include explicit apologies.
- **Build:** 183 cm; athletic, upright, long torso; movement remains controlled even while injured.
- **Face:** narrow diamond shape; cool olive skin; pale cyan-gray eyes; sharply angled dark brows; long straight nose; thin vertical scar through the left eyebrow; clean-shaven.
- **Hair:** ink-black, straight, shoulder length, fixed low half-tail fastened with a matte silver clasp; one loose strand crosses the right cheek.
- **Clothing:** layered deep-teal tunic beneath graphite scale-and-cloth travel armor; asymmetrical silver shoulder guard on the left; muted cyan piping; black gloves; dark boots; pale Veyran cloak lined in blue-gray. Armor is practical and worn, never royal gold.
- **Props:** narrow broken scabbard with no usable blade during most of the story; silver oath cylinder; indigo crystal-wrapping cloth.
- **Posture and expression:** straight-backed; left hand often protects the wounded ribs; eye contact is unwavering; guilt shows as a lowered chin rather than tears.
- **Portrait framing:** chest-up, three-quarter toward center, left shoulder guard and eyebrow scar visible; cool cyan rim light.
- **Sprite silhouette:** slim tall graphite/teal body, pale cloak wedge, black half-tail, one silver shoulder pixel/block.
- **Invariants:** cyan-gray eyes; left-eyebrow vertical scar; black shoulder-length half-tail with silver clasp; loose right-cheek strand; deep teal and graphite armor; single left shoulder guard; cool olive skin.
- **Allowed changes:** rib bandages at Rowanstead; cloak removed indoors; frost and torn cloak at Frostcrown; controlled expressions.
- **Expression set:** guarded neutral, pained, skeptical, determined, guilty, faint relief.
- **Reusable generation description:** “Sir Cael Varin, a lean athletic 37-year-old Veyran knight with cool olive skin, narrow diamond face, pale cyan-gray eyes, sharply angled brows, a thin vertical scar through the left eyebrow, long straight nose, and ink-black shoulder-length hair in a low half-tail with a matte silver clasp, one loose strand across his right cheek. He wears deep-teal layers under worn graphite scale-and-cloth travel armor, a single silver left shoulder guard, muted cyan piping, black gloves, dark boots, and a pale blue-gray-lined cloak. Grounded anime-inspired painted 2D.”
- **Negative constraints:** no blond hair, beard, blue plate armor, second shoulder guard, gold crown, huge sword, glowing eyes, smiling hero pose, hair redesign, text, watermark.

## Mira Fen

- **Identity:** she/her; Greenwake apprentice healer and evacuation leader; 23.
- **Personality and motivation:** pragmatic, warm without being sentimental, quick under pressure, and unimpressed by titles. She joins only if the player prioritizes civilians. Her goal is to keep ordinary people visible in decisions made by kings and seers.
- **Relationships:** becomes Aren’s frank peer; trusts Bram quickly; challenges Cael’s “necessary sacrifice” language; helps Elara in ending three; admires Mara’s hands more than her airship.
- **Speaking style:** brisk questions, concrete medical observations, wry corrections. Uses people’s names frequently.
- **Build:** 166 cm; compact, strong legs and forearms; balanced stance.
- **Face:** rounded heart shape; light golden-brown skin; large dark hazel eyes; curved brows; small upturned nose; one beauty mark beneath the right eye.
- **Hair:** dense dark auburn curls to the jaw, held back on the left by two fixed brass pins; never straight or long.
- **Clothing:** cream wrap blouse; deep jade sleeveless apron-coat with split tails; cinnamon sash with herb pouches; charcoal leggings; ankle boots; rolled linen bandages at the belt.
- **Props:** jade-glass medicine vials, folding herb knife used as a tool, canvas field pouch.
- **Posture and expression:** hands on hips when impatient; kneels to speak with injured people; smile is open and asymmetric.
- **Portrait framing:** chest-up, toward center, brass pins and herb pouch strap visible.
- **Sprite silhouette:** round auburn hair, jade split coat, cream sleeves, cinnamon waist.
- **Invariants:** jaw-length dark auburn curls; two brass pins on left; beauty mark below right eye; jade apron-coat; cinnamon sash; herb pouches.
- **Allowed changes:** soot, rolled sleeves, healer’s white over-cloak at ending, emotions.
- **Expression set:** focused neutral, warm smile, worried, determined, surprised, irritated.
- **Reusable generation description:** “Mira Fen, a compact 23-year-old fantasy healer with light golden-brown skin, rounded heart-shaped face, large dark-hazel eyes, curved brows, small upturned nose, beauty mark beneath her right eye, and dense jaw-length dark-auburn curls held on the left by exactly two brass pins. Cream wrap blouse, deep-jade sleeveless split-tail apron-coat, cinnamon sash with herb pouches, charcoal leggings and ankle boots. Grounded anime-inspired painted 2D.”
- **Negative constraints:** no long straight hair, blond hair, nurse uniform, white dress, staff, weapon pose, pale blue palette, missing brass pins, text, watermark.

## Mara “Cinder” Vell

- **Identity:** she/her; inventor of Asterra’s first working airship, the **Starling**; 39. “Cinder” is the nickname stamped on workshop tools, inspired by the source’s tentative “Cid.”
- **Personality and motivation:** restless, brilliant, theatrical about machines, quietly furious that Edric weaponized her work. She helps Lucen’s resistance because invention should make distance smaller, not targets easier to reach.
- **Relationships:** old friend and occasional scolder of Lucen; respects Bram’s tool sense; calls Aren “Mushroom”; immediately argues with Cael about magical drain rates.
- **Speaking style:** clipped technical metaphors followed by sudden lyrical enthusiasm. Finishes other people’s mechanical explanations, never their emotional ones.
- **Build:** 171 cm; rangy; long arms; narrow hips; perpetual forward tilt.
- **Face:** long oval; deep umber skin; bright copper-brown eyes; high arched brows; strong narrow nose; diagonal pale burn scar along the right cheek.
- **Hair:** silver-gray, coarse and voluminous, chin length on the right and shaved close on the left; fixed dark goggles rest on the crown.
- **Clothing:** graphite sleeveless mechanic’s coat over Ember-orange high-collar shirt; dark leather bracers; many-pocketed charcoal trousers; brass-buckled boots; amber thread stitching.
- **Props:** folding brass spanner, graphite goggles with amber lenses, chalk tablet, finger caps on left hand.
- **Posture and expression:** points with tools; leans into personal space when excited; grin shows one slightly chipped front tooth.
- **Portrait framing:** chest-up, shaved left side and cheek burn visible, goggles fully in frame.
- **Sprite silhouette:** asymmetric silver hair, amber goggles, graphite long coat, orange chest.
- **Invariants:** deep umber skin; asymmetrical silver hair with left side shaved; right-cheek diagonal burn; crown goggles with amber lenses; graphite coat; Ember shirt; brass spanner.
- **Allowed changes:** welding mask pushed up; soot; flight scarf and coat fastened at Frostcrown.
- **Expression set:** appraising neutral, broad grin, worried calculation, fierce determination, surprise, anger.
- **Reusable generation description:** “Mara ‘Cinder’ Vell, a rangy 39-year-old fantasy airship engineer with deep umber skin, long oval face, copper-brown eyes, arched brows, strong narrow nose, diagonal pale burn scar on the right cheek, and coarse voluminous silver-gray hair chin-length on the right with the left side shaved. Graphite goggles with amber lenses on her crown, graphite sleeveless mechanic’s coat, Ember-orange high-collar shirt, dark bracers, many-pocketed trousers, brass-buckled boots, folding brass spanner. Grounded anime-inspired painted 2D.”
- **Negative constraints:** no steampunk top hat, blond hair, symmetrical hairstyle, pale skin, lab coat, firearm, overly sexualized outfit, missing goggles or scar, text, watermark.

## King Edric Aurel

- **Identity:** he/him; king of Asterra and central antagonist; 52.
- **Personality and motivation:** once conscientious and restrained, now sleep-deprived, controlling, and terrifyingly willing to treat lives as time he cannot spare. He believes Veyra closed the gate to deny his dying wife a cure. Love explains his actions but does not excuse them.
- **Relationships:** devoted to Elara; estranged from Lucen; considers Cael an enemy agent; initially dismisses Aren as a bereaved child and later recognizes his own obsession in him.
- **Speaking style:** formal royal plural in public, intimate fragments when discussing Elara, sharp commands when plans slip. Never cackles or boasts.
- **Build:** 186 cm; imposing but slightly gaunt; broad shoulders under a heavy cloak.
- **Face:** long square face; light olive skin with exhausted shadows; steel-blue eyes; thick dark-gray brows; aquiline nose; close-trimmed salt-and-pepper beard; fixed small scar at the left corner of the mouth.
- **Hair:** dark brown heavily streaked silver, swept straight back to the collar; neat, never loose.
- **Clothing:** charcoal ceremonial military coat with restrained antique-gold piping, deep wine-red sash, black gloves, high boots, graphite half-cape lined burgundy; narrow blackened-silver circlet rather than a large crown.
- **Props:** signet ring engraved with a seven-point circle; Queen Elara’s pressed moonflower in a glass locket; ceremonial straight sword used only within cut-scenes.
- **Posture and expression:** rigid; one hand closes around the flower locket; exhaustion briefly softens his eyes before anger returns.
- **Portrait framing:** chest-up, slightly low angle, circlet, wine sash, and locket visible; Ember backlight, cold face light.
- **Sprite silhouette:** tall dark cape, silver hair line, wine sash diagonal, narrow circlet.
- **Invariants:** swept-back brown-and-silver hair; steel-blue eyes; salt-and-pepper beard; mouth-corner scar; narrow black-silver circlet; charcoal coat; wine sash; glass flower locket.
- **Allowed changes:** cape removed in Veyra; cracked circlet at climax; frost, exhaustion, and minor wounds.
- **Expression set:** controlled neutral, weary tenderness, suspicion, command, fury, defeated shock.
- **Reusable generation description:** “King Edric Aurel, an imposing gaunt 52-year-old fantasy king with light olive skin, long square face, exhausted steel-blue eyes, thick dark-gray brows, aquiline nose, close salt-and-pepper beard, small scar at the left mouth corner, and dark-brown collar-length hair heavily streaked silver and swept straight back. Narrow blackened-silver circlet, charcoal military coat with restrained antique-gold piping, deep wine-red sash, black gloves, graphite half-cape lined burgundy, glass locket holding a pressed pale moonflower. Grounded anime-inspired painted 2D.”
- **Negative constraints:** no villain horns, black spiked armor, giant crown, glowing red eyes, bald head, clean-shaven face, purple robes, skull imagery, manic grin, missing locket, text, watermark.

## Queen Elara Aurel

- **Identity:** she/her; queen of Asterra; 50.
- **Personality and motivation:** thoughtful former natural philosopher, empathetic, politically direct. Illness leaves her physically weak but not passive. Her private notes argue against invasion even if Veyran medicine could save her.
- **Relationships:** loves Edric but condemns what he does in her name; mother of Lucen and early patron of Mara’s work; she survives only in the Open Sky ending.
- **Speaking style:** gentle precise language, botanical metaphors, questions that expose evasions.
- **Build:** 169 cm; slender, visibly frail during the story.
- **Face:** elegant oval; warm beige skin; soft green eyes; fine arched brows; straight narrow nose; faint smile lines; no scars.
- **Hair:** long honey-brown hair threaded with silver, fixed in a low braided coil with one pale moonflower pin.
- **Clothing:** layered dove-gray and muted sage gown, high soft collar, fine amber embroidery of seven leaves, charcoal shawl.
- **Props:** moonflower hairpin; botanical notebook; slim wedding band.
- **Posture and expression:** supported upright rather than reclining helplessly; hands folded around notebook; tired half-smile.
- **Portrait framing:** shoulders and head, soft window light, moonflower pin visible.
- **Sprite silhouette:** not used in exploration; cut-scenes only.
- **Invariants:** green eyes; honey-brown/silver low braided coil; moonflower pin; dove-gray and sage layers; amber seven-leaf embroidery.
- **Allowed changes:** bed shawl, healthier complexion in ending three, travel coat in epilogue.
- **Expression set:** calm, fatigued, worried, firm, relieved smile.
- **Reusable generation description:** “Queen Elara Aurel, a frail but composed 50-year-old fantasy queen with warm beige skin, elegant oval face, soft green eyes, fine arched brows, straight narrow nose, faint smile lines, and long honey-brown hair threaded silver in a low braided coil with one pale moonflower pin. Layered dove-gray and muted-sage high-collar gown with fine amber seven-leaf embroidery and charcoal shawl. Grounded anime-inspired painted 2D.”
- **Negative constraints:** no crown, blond hair, loose hairstyle, princess youthfulness, ornate ball gown, helpless sleeping pose, glowing magic, missing flower pin, text, watermark.

## Prince Lucen Aurel

- **Identity:** he/him; crown prince and organizer of a quiet palace resistance; 26.
- **Personality and motivation:** diplomatic, self-critical, principled, and less sheltered than Aren expects. He wants to stop his father without publicly humiliating the people who still trust the crown. He becomes king in endings two and three.
- **Relationships:** son of Edric and Elara; lifelong friend of Mara; wary ally of Cael; sees Aren as a citizen whose moral authority exceeds his rank.
- **Speaking style:** polished but avoids royal plural; apologizes directly; thinks in compromises and asks others to identify their non-negotiable need.
- **Build:** 179 cm; slim, straight posture, graceful rather than martial.
- **Face:** tapered oval; light olive skin; clear green-blue eyes; gently angled brows; straight aquiline nose; no beard; a small dimple in the left cheek.
- **Hair:** dark brown with one fixed silver streak at the right temple, softly waved, jaw length, tucked behind both ears.
- **Clothing:** deep navy long vest over muted cream shirt; graphite fitted coat; restrained Ember cord at the shoulder; dark trousers and boots; no crown until epilogue, then the same narrow circlet repaired.
- **Props:** palace archive key ring; folded civilian petitions; later a plain graphite signet.
- **Posture and expression:** open hands; shoulders drop when speaking about his mother; composed eye contact.
- **Portrait framing:** chest-up, toward center, silver temple streak and Ember cord visible.
- **Sprite silhouette:** slim navy body, dark jaw-length hair, one pale temple mark, Ember shoulder dot.
- **Invariants:** green-blue eyes; dark jaw-length tucked hair; single silver streak at right temple; left-cheek dimple; navy vest; cream shirt; Ember shoulder cord.
- **Allowed changes:** hooded resistance cloak; repaired circlet and formal coat in endings.
- **Expression set:** diplomatic neutral, relieved smile, worried, resolved, surprised, grief.
- **Reusable generation description:** “Prince Lucen Aurel, a slim 26-year-old fantasy crown prince with light olive skin, tapered oval face, clear green-blue eyes, gently angled brows, straight aquiline nose, left-cheek dimple, and softly waved jaw-length dark-brown hair tucked behind both ears with one distinct silver streak at the right temple. Deep-navy long vest over muted-cream shirt, fitted graphite coat, restrained Ember-orange shoulder cord, dark trousers and boots. Grounded anime-inspired painted 2D.”
- **Negative constraints:** no beard, blond hair, long flowing hair, large crown, plate armor, red royal cape, arrogant smirk, missing silver streak or shoulder cord, text, watermark.

## One-scene figures and creatures

These designs may recur as map silhouettes but do not require expression sets.

- **Commander Holt:** Asterra field commander, late 30s, brass-rimmed graphite helmet, square Ember tabard, fully enclosed disciplined silhouette. He embodies the machine of the campaign rather than personal evil. Never confused with Edric.
- **Rowan:** elderly Rowanstead farmer, late 60s, short and round, white braid, indigo work dress, amber lantern.
- **Crystal wardens:** nonhuman manifestations placed by Cael. Each has a dark mineral body broken by amber light, an animal-like regional silhouette, and no clothing, language, gore, or demonic religious symbols. Ashfinger resembles a horned river stag; Cinder Thumb resembles a basalt salamander; Frostcrown resembles a long-winged ice moth.
- **Veyran seer Ilyan:** elderly nonbinary figure seen only in a stylized memory; dark teal layered robes, shaved head, cyan geometric freckles, closed silver eyes. Their prophecy is presented as sincere but fallible interpretation, not omniscient fact.
