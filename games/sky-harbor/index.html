YOUR FIRST FLIGHT

A little power. A gentle touch.

Start with Haven Coast → Meadow Field. Both runways face east: take off, climb and continue straight toward the gold approach gates.

  1. Power: hold W for 3.4 seconds to reach 100%. Release the key to keep the setting. Wait until 40 m/s.
  2. Rotate & climb: hold ↓ for about 0.9 seconds to set pitch +8°. Release: pitch stays where you put it. Climb to the displayed altitude, then hold ↑ until pitch reads 0°.
  3. Cruise: hold S to reduce power to 76%. A/D (or ←/→) bank and turn. Release to level the wings; pitch does not auto-level. Small taps help.
  4. Arrival: the map points to a gate 3.2 km before the runway. Line up with the runway heading. Reduce power to 40% and speed to 40–46 m/s. Trim pitch to the displayed whole-degree target. Gold gates show the descent path.
  5. Flare: at 7 m AGL, tap ↓ to +1° pitch and hold S to idle. Keep the wings level and let the aircraft settle. After touchdown, hold Space to stop on the runway.

Too high, fast or sideways? Full power, pitch +8°, climb 300 m above the airport, turn around and return to the gate. No timer or fuel limit.

Training assists: calm wind, coordinated turns, wing leveling on release, pitch trim that stays set, and generous lift above 40 m/s. Lift fades below 40; lower the nose and add power if slow. Fixed gear and fixed landing-lift configuration; no flap or rudder keys. No steering, rotation, flare or landing is automatic.

ASL = altitude above sea level. AGL = wheel clearance above local terrain/water. Hard landing limits: descent 4.5 m/s, speed 54 m/s, bank 13°, heading error 15°. Stop at the chosen airport in its selected runway direction to complete the route.
