# Generated image manifest

All eight shipped scenes were generated specifically for `Deep sea journey`
with OpenAI's image-generation tool, then exported locally as 1600×900 WebP at
quality 86. No remote image URL is used at runtime.

## Shared art direction

Cinematic natural-history illustration for young children; scientifically
recognizable animals and habitats; realistic water optics, suspended
particulates, depth, and restrained painterly detail; wonder without menace;
wide 16:9 composition; no captions, labels, borders, logos, signatures,
watermarks, or pseudo-text. Subjects were kept away from hard screen edges and
visual detail was distributed so both left- and right-aligned panels and mobile
crops retain a useful focal area.

These are artist interpretations, not documentary photographs, anatomical
plates, or scale diagrams. Relative size, animal proximity, visibility, and
lighting are composed for storytelling. Scene depth labels and scientific
claims come from the researched story, not from the image pixels alone.

## Selection and revisions

Ten generation outputs were evaluated to ship eight final scenes. The first
abyssal and vent attempts were retained only in the generation workspace, not
the application. Targeted image edits produced the two corrected WebP assets
described above. Final images were visually inspected after WebP conversion.
