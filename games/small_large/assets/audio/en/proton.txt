Zoom out one hundred thousand times and a structure appears: the proton. Even now, do not imagine a neat container with three beads inside. A proton is a storm of quantum fields. Three valence quarks establish its electric charge, while gluons and short-lived quark pairs surge through the same tiny region. Its recommended charge radius is about zero point eight four femtometres. Much of the proton’s mass comes not from adding quark masses, but from the energy of this confined motion. The apparently solid world begins with something astonishingly restless.
