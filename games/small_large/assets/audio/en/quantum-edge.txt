Begin before anything has a familiar surface. In our best-tested model, quarks and electrons are fundamental and point-like. That does not prove they are infinitely small. It means experiments have found no inner structure. In 2026, CERN reported quarks still looking point-like down to about ten to the minus twenty metres. Here, the light is an artist’s language for interaction. Our first step is therefore not a measured diameter, but an honest frontier: the smallest scale we have directly tested.
