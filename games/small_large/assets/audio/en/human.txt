Here is the scale your intuition calls normal: roughly a metre or two, the span of a person. It is tempting to call this the middle, but nature has no obligation to centre itself on us. Human scale is simply our balcony. From here, a virus is invisible, sand is small, a whale is enormous, and the stars look like points. We build doors, tools, music, and stories around bodies of this size. The journey has already crossed about twenty powers of ten to reach us. Now turn around. The outward half begins under the same sky.
