The Sun does not stop at its glowing surface. It exhales a solar wind of charged particles that inflates a vast, changing region called the heliosphere. Far beyond the planets, that wind slows and meets interstellar material at the heliopause. Voyager One crossed into interstellar plasma in 2012 at roughly one hundred twenty-two times the Earth–Sun distance; Voyager Two followed in 2018 along another route. Calling this the edge of the solar system is convenient, not final. The Sun’s gravity reaches farther, and the boundary itself breathes with solar activity and the interstellar environment.
