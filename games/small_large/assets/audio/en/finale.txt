Return to the figure under the stars. Nothing here is merely in the middle. Your body contains cells; cells arrange molecules; molecules are built from atoms; atoms carry protons; and those protons seethe with fields. Above you, Earth moves inside the Sun’s breath, one star crosses the Milky Way, and our galaxy shares space with giants whose edges fade into uncertainty. We crossed more than forty-two powers of ten, but size never became importance. The smallest shapes the largest, and the largest gives the smallest a place. Take one breath. You are not outside the scale of things. You are one of its temporary, wondering arrangements.
