# Generated artwork manifest

These 18 raster assets were made specifically for **THE LONG ZOOM** with the built-in image-generation tool, visually reviewed as a complete contact sheet, centre-cropped/downscaled with Sharp, and exported as 1600×900 WebP at quality 86. Generated PNG originals remain in the Codex generated-image store; the WebP files here are the runtime deliverables.

## Shared art direction and prompt block

Every individual prompt included the following production direction, with a scene-specific scientific request appended:

> Full-screen 16:9 scene artwork for a cinematic scientific scale journey. Premium cinematic scientific visualization or naturalistic photography with material realism and a coherent visual-essay art direction. Deep layered space; focal subject within the central 40 percent, safe under translucent side panels and a mobile centre crop. Deep Graphite shadows, restrained Ember rim light, Golden Amber energy highlights, and subject-specific cyan, jade, ocean blue, or indigo. No text, labels, numbers, captions, arrows, borders, flags, logos, signatures, watermarks, or interface elements.

No image is presented as a scale diagram. Absolute measurements are supplied in the localized story; the artwork provides a memorable subject and spatial feeling.
