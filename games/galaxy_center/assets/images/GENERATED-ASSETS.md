# Generated image manifest

All nine images were generated specifically for this experience on 2026-07-29
with the built-in image generation tool. Accepted outputs were resized to
1600×900 and encoded as WebP quality 86 with Sharp. No third-party image is
loaded at runtime.

Shared direction: data-informed cinematic observatory visualization; deep
Graphite space; restrained Ember and Golden Amber warmth; cyan and magenta
scientific-signal accents; rich depth; readable central crop; no text, labels,
watermarks, signatures, invented spacecraft, or pseudo-writing.

The files are artist interpretations, not photographs, scale diagrams, or
literal naked-eye views. Multiwavelength scenes translate infrared, radio, and
X-ray evidence into visible color.
