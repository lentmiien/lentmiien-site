Time for our second discovery.

Earth gives us one familiar clock by circling the Sun. Our whole Solar System gives us a much larger clock by circling the center of the Milky Way.

How large is that galactic clock? Look at the choices. Is one galactic year about two hundred and thirty days, two hundred and thirty thousand Earth years, or two hundred and thirty million Earth years?

Choose the scale that feels right for a journey across an enormous galaxy. You can use the panel or press one, two, or three. Whether your first guess is near or far, the explanation will help stretch your sense of cosmic time.
