    About this journey

    Controls

    Use the arrows below, the route points, or the ← and → keys to move. Number keys 1–3 answer a discovery.

    Press R to replay narration, C for the transcript, M for sound, and P for the story panel. Escape closes the transcript or this dialog.

    Hiding the panel leaves navigation, sound, transcript, help, and progress available. Discoveries open the panel automatically and restore your preference on the next scene.

    Media & accessibility

    Every scene image was generated for this experience. They are cinematic artist interpretations informed by current observations—not photographs, cockpit views, or scale diagrams. Invisible wavelengths are translated into visible color.

    English narration was made locally with the Piper Lessac voice. Browser-ready MP3 files, exact transcripts, and WAV masters are stored with the app; no media service is contacted at runtime.

    Decorative motion follows your reduced-motion setting. Narration starts only after the launch button, and the transcript opens independently of the story panel.

    Research sources

    Facts were checked against these NASA, ESA, and ESO resources on July 29, 2026. Distances and galaxy dimensions are rounded for young listeners, and scientific models continue to improve.
