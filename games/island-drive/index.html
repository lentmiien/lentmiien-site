Slow before corners. Comet carries much more speed and runs wide if you turn too late. Off-road grass reduces speed and grip. Reverse to get away from an obstacle.

Quality = on-road distance percentage − 5 per collision. Clamped to 0–100. Standing still earns nothing; one continuous contact counts once. Your car cannot break.

Four gold markers sit on reachable roads. Drive through one to move it to another location. Discoveries are exploration only: no points, speed boosts or resources.

Only water ends the session. There is no finish line, timer, fuel or damage. Your statistics last until you restart or leave.

On touch devices, hold the on-screen steering and pedal buttons. Landscape orientation gives the clearest view. Sound is optional; all messages are also visual.
