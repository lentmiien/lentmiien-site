⚔ CIVILIZATION

Build your empire from 4000 BC to the modern age.
A 4X strategy game inspired by the classics.

VICTORY!

How to Play

  • Expand: move a Settler to open land, then press Found City.
  • Develop: cities produce food (growth), shields (build units & buildings) and science (research).
  • Conquer: select a military unit, click a blue tile to move, or click an enemy unit to attack. Attacking starts a war — or declare one in Diplomacy.
  • Research: click an available tech in the Technology panel to research it next. Techs unlock new units and buildings. The panel has three display modes — HIDDEN (only era titles; click one to expand its tech list), SELECTABLE (only the techs you can research now) and ALL (the entire tree).
  • Win: destroy every other civilization's cities. If you lose all yours, your civilization falls.
  • Gold flows in from gold resources and markets. While at peace, a rival can be offered a 100-gold gift (Diplomacy) — gift-giving makes them considerably less likely to declare war on you.
  • Domains: land units cannot enter water; ships cannot enter land; only planes fly over everything. Mountains block land & sea — only planes cross them.
  • Crossing the sea: the Ship, Transport Ship, War Galley and Carrack carry one land unit. Select a land unit standing on the coast next to a friendly transport — its panel shows a ⛴ Board button (you can also click the highlighted blue water tile). Then sail the ship across and use ⚓ Disembark (or click a highlighted shore tile) to step ashore. A passenger cannot fight or found cities while aboard, and is lost if the ship sinks. When a unit is aboard, clicking the stack selects the boat first, so you can keep sailing it.
  • Stacking: your units may share a square (including your own city). When several are stacked, use the “Units here” list to pick one — carrier ships are listed first.
  • Roads: select a Settler (or an Engineer, once unlocked) and press ⛏ Build Road to lay a road on that tile. Roads cut land movement cost to 1, so your army moves faster along them.
  • Wonders: the Great Pyramid, Great Wall, Colossus, Hanging Gardens, Moai, Lighthouse of Alexandria and National Museum can each be built once per game. The first city to finish one claims it, and its bonus then applies to all of your cities. Claimed wonders are greyed out in other cities.
  • Production carry-over: if you switch a city's production, the shields already invested are kept and applied to the new item — a Settler at 15/30 becomes a Warrior at 15/18. If the carried shields already exceed the new item's cost, it finishes at the end of this turn and the excess is discarded — but until the turn ends you may still switch to something more expensive to keep them.
  • Rankings: the World Rankings panel shows which civilization is leading (cities, units, techs, wonders).
  • Hover any explored tile to see what that terrain does (yields, movement cost, defense).
  • Sound: the 🔊 button in the top bar toggles the retro bleeps.
  • Drag the map to pan, mouse wheel to zoom, Enter to end the turn.
