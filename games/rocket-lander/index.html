THE SURVEY PILOT’S FIELD NOTES

Small burns. Soft feet.

  1. Rise before you turn. Use short burns of W, ↑ or Space to fire the main engine. Watch HEIGHT and release early to coast upward; a long burn can carry you beyond the sector ceiling. Clear the ridge heights before crossing. The dashed line marks the sector edge.
  2. Tilt with A / D or ← / →. A fires the right nozzle to tilt left. D fires the left nozzle to tilt right. Release a turn early: angular damping slows spin, but does not level the rocket. Opposing turn buttons cancel without spending fuel.
  3. Brake before the goal. Tilt against your horizontal motion and burn. Return upright, then use short main-engine pulses to slow your descent. There is gentle linear drag; releasing the engine never holds altitude.
  4. Land on the gold ARRIVAL pad. Both feet must be inside its lights. Touch down descending at ≤ 3.4 m/s, sideways at ≤ 2.5 m/s, tilt ≤ 12°, spin ≤ 20°/s. Safe contact settles the landing gear for 0.6 seconds. Blue DEPARTURE allows a safe return, but earns no result.

160 units per standard flight. Main burn: 1.60 units/s; each rotation engine: 0.28 units/s. Zero fuel in flight ends the attempt. A safe arrival on the same physics step counts before depletion. No time or distance bonus.
