KEEP YOUR BEARINGS

White above. Green in front.

Start with the white center on top and the green center facing you. Red is on your right. Face letters refer to this fixed frame, even when you orbit the view.

Choose a color, then click a sticker. Each square is labeled with its color initial. Centers cannot change.

  • F, R, B, L: face that center toward you, keeping white on top. Read left to right.
  • U: tip the top toward you; blue is at the top of the grid, green at the bottom.
  • D: tip the bottom toward you; green is at the top of the grid, blue at the bottom.

Different color scheme? Map your center colors to this reference frame, then use that mapping for every sticker.

A quick guide to cube notation

U = top, R = right, F = front, D = bottom, L = left, B = back. A plain letter is a clockwise quarter turn, a prime (′) reverses it, and 2 means a half turn. Always judge clockwise while looking directly at the face being turned, including the back and bottom.

For example, R U R′ U′ turns the right clockwise, top clockwise, right counterclockwise, then top counterclockwise. Only turn the indicated layer; keep the whole physical cube in its starting orientation.
